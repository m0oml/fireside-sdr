# FT8: our decoder vs WSJT-X (jt9), live capture 2026-11-17

Capture: 7.074 MHz FT8, ANT1 (wire), 10 min (`recordings/live/20261117_233444_7074_FT8_ant1_48k_8ch.wav`, not in git), started 23:34:44.6 UTC just before a slot boundary.
Radio: DDS 9 kHz below the dial, I/Q mirrored (both undone by `tools/compare_ft8.py`). jt9 = WSJT-X 3.3.0-beta5 (`-8 -d 3`). Reproduce: `tools/compare_ft8.py <capture.wav>`.

## Result (36 slots)
| | decodes |
|---|---|
| WSJT-X jt9 | 834 (about 23 per slot) |
| ours | 497 |
| both | 497 |
| only jt9 | 337 |
| only ours | **0** |

* **No false decodes**: every message we decoded was also decoded by jt9. Frequency agrees to within 3 Hz (mean +0.1 Hz).
* **We find 59.6 %** of what jt9 finds, by jt9's SNR: > -10 dB: 304/349 (87 %); -10..-15: 144/264 (55 %); -15..-20: 44/173 (25 %); <= -20: 5/48 (10 %). Expected: ft8_lib is single-pass without a-priori decoding; jt9 at depth 3 subtracts decoded signals and tries again. Weak-signal sensitivity is where the gap is; strong signals are fine.
* **SNR scale is about 6 dB low**: ours minus jt9 = -5.6 dB mean, -6.0 median, sd 2.8 (live). The same check on WSJT-X's own sample (`recordings/ft8/181201_180245`) gave -7.0 (n = 8). So it is a general offset of our SNR figure (about -6 to -7 dB), not a radio effect. 
* **DT** differs by +0.68 s (ours is measured from the slot start, WSJT-X's DT is relative to 0.5 s after it; the remaining ~0.2 s is unexplained: our decoder's offset or the capture trim). Display only.

## Update: SNR recalibrated (2026-11-18)
`kSnrCalibrationDb` in `core/src/decode_ft8.cpp` went from -4.0 to **+1.7** dB. Re-run on the same capture: ours - jt9 = **+0.1 dB mean** (median 0.0, sd 2.9); the decode count is unchanged (497, zero false). The error was flat across the range (-5.5 to -6.2 dB from -14 to +8 dB SNR, fit slope 0.95), so a constant is the right correction.
Caveat: the old constant was tuned to 2019 WSJT-X reports on four quiet-band recordings. jt9 3.3 reads +2.7 dB above those reports on the same files, and on those quiet recordings our new figure is about +3 dB high; on the busy live band (crowded neighbours raise our noise estimate) it is right. So our noise estimator is band-dependent by about 3 dB: a better (median-based) noise estimate would fix both, not done. Tests: `ft8_snr_matches_current_wsjtx_on_a_busy_band` (two live slots + jt9 references in `app/tests/ft8/`, fails with the old constant) and the 2019-recordings test now uses jt9 3.3 references with a +/-4 dB tolerance.

## Not tested
Other bands and times, FT4, and what lowers our weak-signal rate (candidate count, passes, subtraction). The receive chain itself (radio, I/Q, mirror, IF offset, timing) is not the limit.

- Rate is not a factor: the same 192 kHz capture decimated to 48 kHz gives ours 532 decodes against 531 (and a 48 kHz chunk upsampled to 192 kHz gave
  identical messages). The lower strong-signal share is the busier band (more overlapping signals; we do no subtraction), not the 192 kHz path.
- Script fix: jt9 decodes each 15 s file alone and prints an unknown hashed call as `<...>`, ours carries its hash table and prints `<CALL>`;
