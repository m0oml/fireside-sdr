# FT8 decoder: multi-pass signal subtraction (2026-11-18)

Our decoder (ft8_lib-based, `app/core/src/decode_ft8.cpp`) now decodes in up to 3 passes. After each pass every newly decoded message is rebuilt
from its tones (same GFSK pulse as WSJT-X), fitted to the audio (coarse-to-fine search over start sample and frequency, scored per symbol so fading
does not spoil it), its complex envelope estimated (two cascaded 3000-sample boxcars, edge-normalised) and subtracted; the residual is searched again.

Tuning loop: `tools/ft8bench.py` (fast: decodes the 12 kHz slot files of compare_ft8 work folders against cached jt9 results, ~40 s per setting on 79 slots).

# Deep stage: fine sync, 1/2/3-symbol soft metrics and a-priori decoding (2026-11-18, Opus)
`app/core/src/ft8_demod.cpp` (from WSJT-X ft8_downsample / sync8d / ft8b): for each candidate that both earlier stages failed, mix to 200 Hz baseband, refine time
(+-0.2 s, then +-4 samples) and frequency (+-2.5 Hz in 0.5 Hz) on the Costas arrays, take complex per-symbol spectra, bail out if <= 6 of 21 Costas symbols are right,
and build WSJT-X's five soft-bit sets (1, 2, 3 symbols coherent; bit-normalised; strongest of 1-3). Each is tried with BP + OSD; then the a-priori passes on sets 1 and 3:
"a1" = CQ in the first field, "a2" = your call (Decoder card "my call" box, else the Log tab's Wavelog station; preset to M0OML). AP bits pinned at 1.1 x max |llr|,
excluded from BP updates and from OSD flips. Decodes are marked " a1" / " a2" like WSJT-X (the logbook parser strips the marker).
Plausibility now applied to EVERY decode, as WSJT-X's unpacker does: callok on each full callsign (3+ chars, no leading Q, call-area digit 2nd/3rd, prefix has a letter,
suffix all letters), no "CQ ... R grid", no "CQ ... report/RRR/RR73/73", no "CQ <hash>". Weak/deep decodes of the "/P" (i3 = 2, EU VHF) form are refused: the two
false decodes seen in testing (one live, one in noise) were of that form, and it is not used on HF.

## FT4 on the air, portable stations and false decodes (2026-11-18 evening)

Data: three real FT4 recordings on 40 m made with the new Rec button (7.0474 / 7.0475 MHz, 31 s and 59 s, 192 kHz I/Q), ten 7.5 s slots, jt9 -5 as reference. Before: ours found 3 of jt9's 5
(first recording) and 7 of 9 (second); the misses were mostly messages from a portable station (`EA6SA/P`).

**Two faults, both in our own plausibility code, found with `FT8_DEBUG_DROP=1` (prints why a decoded message was thrown away):**
1. `payload_plausible` read the "R" flag from bit 57 and the grid from bit 58. In the standard message (`ft8_lib` message.c `ftx_message_decode_std`, WSJT-X packjt77) bit 57 is the second call's /R or /P
   flag, "R" is bit 58 and the grid/report is bits 59..73. So `CQ EA6SA/P JM19` (type 2, /P set) looked like "CQ ... R grid" and was refused: **every CQ from a portable station, in FT8 and FT4.**
2. Weak (OSD / deep) decodes of type 2 (/P) were refused outright, because two false decodes of that type had once been seen. A real one (`EA6SA/P EA3FBX JN11`, -21 dB, 18 hard errors) was lost.

**Measured instead of guessed.** 4 x 1500 noise slots per mode (`FT8_NOISE_SEED=n ft8bench [--ft4] --noise 1500`; every decode in noise is false) plus 250 genuine weak decodes in the real captures (jt9 agrees):

| | standard (type 1/2) | non-standard (4) | contest / DXpedition / telemetry / free text |
|---|---|---|---|
| false weak decodes in noise (12000 slots) | 0 | 10 | 18, all with >= 18 hard errors |
| genuine weak decodes in real traffic | 230 | 6 | 0 |

New rule (`kUnusualMaxHard`, `kSlashPMaxHard` in decode_ft8.cpp): a weak decode of an unusual type needs <= 17 hard errors; a weak /P decode <= 21 (false /P in noise: 24..26; the genuine one: 18). Trade-off: a genuinely
weak contest-form decode with more than 17 hard errors is now refused (decodes found by the normal BP stage are untouched; no real weak contest data exists to measure this).
**Held-out check** (seeds 5..8, not used to choose the thresholds): false decodes FT4 22 -> 6 per 6000 slots, FT8 9 -> 4. What is left is mostly hashed non-standard forms ("CQ <...>") and a few with
an implausible DT of about -2 s (a candidate for the next filter). Real decodes unchanged: FT8 live 94.1 % of jt9 (+0.6 dB SNR), FT4 live 10 of 10 (-0.4 dB).
Regression tests: `portable_callsigns_decode_in_cq_and_replies_in_ft4_and_ft8` (synthetic) and `ft4_portable_station_slots_decode_as_wsjtx_does` (two real slots); each fix fails its test when undone.
Earlier "0 false decodes in 1000 noise slots" was one seed (12345); other seeds show about 0.15 % (FT8) and 0.37 % (FT4) per slot before this change.

## FT4 deep stage (2026-11-18 evening, Opus)
FT4 had only ft8_lib's BP and the OSD fallback; FT8's deep stage (ft8b.f90) had no FT4 counterpart. Added WSJT-X's FT4 one (`demodulate_ft4`, ft8_demod.cpp; interoperability

correlated on every 2nd sample, the full three-segment DT search (not a window round the candidate: a late `<...> SP3IPA -09` sat outside +-72 ms), fine search +-5 samples and +-4 Hz,
smax >= 1.2, nsync >= 8 of 16 symbols and >= 20 of 32 sync bits, bit metrics from 1, 2 and 4 coherent symbols (3 sets, normalised, x2.83), a-priori on the 4-symbol set with the FT4
XOR sequence applied. Each FT4 frequency is searched once per pass. A deep decode reports its own time and frequency; its waterfall place for the SNR is mapped back with the measured lag
(ft8_lib's waterfall time is 1.24 symbols after the true start: FT8 0.198 s n = 231, FT4 0.059 s n = 21; deep times agree with fitted times within 3 ms).
